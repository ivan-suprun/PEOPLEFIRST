01 Who we are

Clothes for real life, made in runs small enough to count.

Founded by siblings Ivan and Sonya Suprun, both long-time insiders of the fashion industry. Their aim: to prove that well-made basics are timeless, and that a good wardrobe should be simple to own.

02 The idea

True luxury was never about more.

It was always about replacing complexity with clarity. People First designs for the realities of everyday life: pieces that flatter many bodies, are easy to care for and made to last, so that fashion serves both the wearer and the world.

“People are at the centre of everything we do. We pay our artisans the real cost of making, not a cost cut down by mass production.”

Sonya Suprun, Co-founder & CEO

04 How it is made

Made in numbers you can count.

Fabric
Italian deadstock. Fine fabric left over from larger productions. Less waste, and every run is limited by the cloth itself, which gives each piece a one-of-a-kind touch.
Makers
Small ateliers, fair cost. Shirts shaped by the hands of true professionals, using time-honoured techniques, and paid the real cost of the work.
Number
Numbered, never mass. Each customer receives a personally numbered piece, with a textured postcard stating how many were made in that exact pattern. A garment to pass on.
05 The debut collection

Essentials, done properly.

The pieces people actually wear: tailored shirts with buttoned cutouts, comfortable cotton suits, water-resistant outerwear, classic T-shirts and long sleeves.

The signature shirt

Premium deadstock wool, in strictly limited quantities. Each one is personally numbered and arrives with a postcard naming how many were made in that exact pattern.

06 The founders

Ivan & Sonya Suprun

Brother and sister · Founder & Creative Director · Co-founder & CEO

Both built their careers in the fashion industry before starting People First together, with one aim: to simplify the art of dressing.

Ivan Suprun Founder & Creative Director

“After working with clothes for more than fifteen years, I can confidently say that there is no need to implement rocket science into your designs. Everything needs to be understandable, and clothes are no exception. I wanted to create a brand where you can buy five pieces and be fully prepared for any occasion.”
